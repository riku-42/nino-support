PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Applies to NINO 1.0.0
阅读简体中文版本 ↓

Your ocean observations stay on your device.

NINO is maintained by the independent developer identified as riku-42. NINO does not require an account and contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives your app usage or personal information.

Information stored locally

The app saves downloaded public NOAA observations, the times of update attempts, and preferences such as automatic syncing and animations in its own device storage. These are used to display observations offline and schedule future checks. They are not uploaded to the developer. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; restoring an iOS backup may restore data managed by iOS.

Network requests and third parties

When syncing, NINO connects directly over HTTPS to NOAA Climate Prediction Center public data files at www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov. These requests do not include an account, advertising identifier, location, or app-usage analytics added by NINO. As with any internet request, the service and network infrastructure can receive your IP address and standard connection/request information. NOAA processes requests under its own privacy policy.

Automatic syncing can be switched off in Settings. Manual syncing is also available. Background execution is controlled by iOS.

Live Activities and device permissions

NINO uses Apple's Live Activity system to display public ocean observations on your Lock Screen and, on compatible devices, Dynamic Island. NINO has no developer-operated push server. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. You can end a Live Activity in the app or with system controls.

Support and website

Opening external links uses the relevant third-party service. These public support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and do not add analytics scripts, forms, or cookies; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. If you voluntarily open a GitHub support issue, its contents and your GitHub profile are public. Do not include private information in an issue. If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the content you send are used to respond to your request and are not used for advertising.

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve and keep a record of your request. You can ask to delete your support correspondence by emailing allenlzt@icloud.com. Public GitHub issues and service logs are also subject to the relevant service's retention and deletion controls.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the allenlzt@icloud.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日 · 适用于 NINO 1.0.0

NINO 由独立开发者 riku-42 维护。App 无需注册，不含广告、跟踪或第三方统计 SDK。开发者没有用于接收 App 使用情况或个人信息的服务器。

保存在本机的信息

App 在自己的设备存储中保存下载的公开 NOAA 观测、更新尝试时间，以及自动同步和动画等偏好，用于离线展示和安排后续检查。这些信息不会上传给开发者。卸载 App 会移除其本地数据；恢复 iOS 备份时，系统管理的数据可能被恢复。

网络请求与第三方

同步时，NINO 通过 HTTPS 直接访问 www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov 上的 NOAA Climate Prediction Center 公开数据文件。NINO 不会在请求中添加账户、广告标识符、位置或使用统计。与其他互联网请求一样，接收服务和网络基础设施可能获得 IP 地址和标准连接或请求信息。NOAA 按其自身隐私政策处理请求。

你可以在设置中关闭自动同步，或选择手动同步。后台执行由 iOS 决定。

实时活动与设备权限

NINO 通过 Apple 的实时活动系统在锁屏及兼容设备的灵动岛展示公开海洋观测，没有开发者运营的推送服务器。App 不请求位置、通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风权限。你可以在 App 内或通过系统控件结束实时活动。

支持与网站

外部链接由相应第三方服务处理。本支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages，没有添加统计脚本、表单或 Cookie；GitHub 可能按其隐私声明处理技术请求信息。如果你自愿提交 GitHub 支持 issue，其内容和你的 GitHub 资料会公开，请勿提交私人信息。通过邮件联系时，你的邮箱地址及主动发送的内容仅用于处理支持请求，不用于广告。

支持邮件仅保留处理请求及留存必要处理记录所需的时间。你可以向 allenlzt@icloud.com 发送邮件，要求删除你的支持通信。公开 GitHub issue 和服务日志同时受相应服务的数据保留及删除规则管理。

变更与联系

本政策变更后会在本页更新生效日期。隐私问题请通过 allenlzt@icloud.com 联系开发者。
