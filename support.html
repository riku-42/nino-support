How fresh are the observations?

NINO displays NOAA-published weekly relative sea-surface temperature anomalies and three-month RONI values. An observation date identifies the published period, not the time you opened the app. The globe is an illustration rather than a measured temperature map.

When does NINO sync?

With automatic syncing enabled, the app checks when due after a successful check (12 hours) or a failed attempt (1 hour). iOS decides when background work can run, so these intervals are not guaranteed delivery times. You can also use Settings → Sync NOAA now. NINO does not provide server-driven background push notifications.

How do I start Dynamic Island monitoring?

Tap the wave icon at the top right, or open Settings → Dynamic Island & Lock Screen, then choose Start island monitoring. Return to the Home Screen and touch and hold Dynamic Island to expand it. A compatible iPhone is required for Dynamic Island; Lock Screen Live Activities are available on supported iPhones. Activities are time-limited by iOS.

Why is the reading cached or pending?

If NOAA cannot be reached, NINO keeps an existing local or bundled observation and marks its freshness. Check the observation date and your network, then try syncing from Settings. A sync can succeed without new observations if NOAA has not published a new value.

Animations and accessibility

Turn off Ocean animations in Settings to reduce decoration. The app respects iOS Reduce Motion; Dynamic Island animations also pause in Always On mode. Tapping the wave replays decoration without changing values or dates.

Data: NOAA CPC. Map outlines: Natural Earth, public domain. NINO is an independent project and is not an official NOAA app.

数据有多新？

NINO 展示 NOAA 发布的周度相对海温异常，以及三个月 RONI 指数。观测日期代表数据所对应的观测时段，不是打开 App 的时间。地球是视觉示意，不是实测海温栅格图。

什么时候同步？

开启自动同步后，成功检查满 12 小时或失败满 1 小时后会在有执行机会时再次尝试。后台执行时间由 iOS 决定，不能保证准时。也可进入「设置 → 立即同步 NOAA」手动检查。NINO 没有服务器驱动的后台推送通知。

如何开启灵动岛？

轻点右上角海浪图标，或进入「设置 → 灵动岛与锁屏」，选择「开启灵动岛监控」。返回主屏幕后，长按灵动岛展开。灵动岛需要兼容的 iPhone；支持的 iPhone 也可以使用锁屏实时活动。活动时长受 iOS 限制。

为什么显示缓存或待更新？

无法连接 NOAA 时，App 会保留本机缓存或随 App 附带的观测，并标示新鲜度。请核对观测日期、网络连接，再从设置中同步。NOAA 尚未发布新观测时，同步成功也可能没有新数值。

动画与无障碍

可在设置中关闭「海洋动画」。App 遵循 iOS「减少动态效果」，灵动岛动画在息屏常亮时也会停用。轻点海浪只会重播装饰动画，不改变观测值和日期。

数据来自 NOAA CPC，海岸线来自 Natural Earth 公共领域数据。NINO 是独立项目，与 NOAA 无隶属关系。
