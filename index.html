OCEAN CLIMATE OBSERVATORY

A small window
into the Pacific.

NINO is an iPhone app for exploring NOAA-published ocean temperature anomalies, weekly trends and the Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI).

Published observations, clearly presented.

Explore four Niño regions, an illustrated globe and Live Activities on compatible iPhones. Observation dates are always shown; data are not instantaneous measurements.

海洋气候观测

关注海洋，理解我们共同的星球。

NINO 是一款 iPhone 海洋气候观测 App，用来查看 NOAA 发布的海温异常、周度趋势与相对海洋尼诺指数 RONI。

支持四个 Niño 海域、示意地球、锁屏实时活动，以及兼容机型上的灵动岛。数据均标示观测日期，并非此刻瞬时测量。
